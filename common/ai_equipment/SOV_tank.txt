# AI tank design for testing

# [sov-cutting-corners-module] Wartime designs come as enable-EXCLUSIVE pairs on ONE shared
# trigger, WA_AI_EQUIPMENT_should_mount_cutting_corners (focus tech + at war): <key> carries
# NOT, <key>_cc carries it plus the module in a free special slot. When it flips true the
# plain design disables, the fielded variant stops matching, and the AI re-creates it WITH
# the module — that redesign only fires while SOV.txt arms land_xp_spend_priority
# id=equipment_variant on the SAME trigger (desire starved to 0.1/day in 05_defines.lua).
# Keep both halves of a pair in sync; 9 designs with full loadouts (T-44, LTTB, IS-7/8...)
# have no free slot and carry no _cc twin.
