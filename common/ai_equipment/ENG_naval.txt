# AI Ship Designs for His Majesty the King

# ==========================================================================================
# [commonwealth-handoff] (2026-08-21, campaign 8c0fea4c, checklist R90) - THE COMMONWEALTH DOMINIONS ARE IN
# THE `available_for` OF FIVE OF THESE TWELVE GROUPS: ENG_destroyers, ENG_escorts,
# ENG_naval_light_cruiser, ENG_naval_mine_sweeper, ENG_naval_mine_layer.  AST CAN NZL RAJ SAF
# MAL BRM.
#
# Why. The dominions run the BRITISH naval tech tree - MEASURED at 1941.6, every one of them
# holds eng_frigate_1..5 and eng_destroyer_1..6 - and were named in NO ai_equipment file at
# all. The generic designs they ARE allowed to use (generic_naval.txt blocks only the seven
# majors) are gated on `has_tech = generic_frigate_1` / `generic_destroyer_*`, and they hold
# ZERO generic naval techs. So they had the role, the hulls, and no design.
#
# The closure test, same save: every minor on the GENERIC tree saturates its yards - SWE 17
# factories on 16 dockyard levels, TUR 21/20, ARG 6/5, BRA 6/5 - while every dominion on the
# BRITISH tree runs exactly ONE, against 6 to 16 levels (RAJ 1/8, AST 1/6, CAN 1/16, NZL 1/7,
# SAF 1/4). 36 idle dockyard levels between them, and a frigate hull costs 80 IC.
#
# WA already calls them escort navies - `WA_AI_CONFIG_is_escort_navy` = minor AND in the
# Allies - and gives them `role_ratio naval_escort 50`, which had nothing to bind to.
#
# Screens, escorts, minelayers/sweepers and light cruisers ONLY. The capital, super-heavy,
# carrier and submarine groups stay ENG-only on purpose: a dominion putting its yards on a
# battleship is the failure this change must not create. Light cruisers were opened by owner
# ruling 2026-08-21 (Canada and Australia fielded them historically); their role_ratio is 10
# against escort 50, so they cannot dominate the mix.
#
# A tag in `available_for` with no matching `has_tech` simply never enables, so MAL and BRM -
# one dockyard each - cost nothing whether or not they inherit the British hulls.
# ==========================================================================================
