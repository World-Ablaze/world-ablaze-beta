# WA AI Templates - THE GARRISON ROLE IS DELIBERATELY GONE (2026-08-19)
#
# There is no `role = garrison` anywhere in common/ai_templates/. The folder is replace_path'd, so
# vanilla's garrison roles are not loaded either: the role does not exist in a WA game, and no
# division template can be classified into it. The AI builds infantry instead.
#
# Why. The engine gives every existing template the role whose ENABLED target it matches best, and a
# template captured by a role the AI wants zero of is decommissioned, never recruited again. SOV's
# "Strelkovaya Diviziya" froze at exactly 87 divisions from 1936 to 1939 that way; ITA and FIN showed
# the same symptom. Two target shapes were tried and neither held: cheap light infantry too small to
# tile a combat width (4f67ea1c0), then heavy infantry sized to the width tiles (a60d5a976, reverted
# after testing). Removing the role removes the capture mechanism itself, which is the only fix that
# does not depend on a match score staying on the right side of a margin.
#
# Removed together with it - these come back together or not at all:
#   - `role_ratio id = garrison` in WA_AI_PRODUCTION_DEFAULT_army_composition.txt and its gate
#     WA_AI_PRODUCTION_build_army_garrison. A share of a role with no target template cannot be
#     filled, so those 5 points stay with infantry.
#   - WA_AI_TEMPLATES_calculate_garrison_template, the WA_GARRISON_TEMPLATE flag, template type
#     code 8 and its scripted localisation. Value range 9000-9999 is free.
#
# NOT removed: occupation garrisons. The AI picks its state-garrison template by scoring all of its
# own templates (NDefines.NAI.GARRISON_TEMPLATE_SCORE_IC_FACTOR / _MANPOWER_FACTOR - lowest cost per
# suppression wins), not by the ai_templates role, so occupied territory is still garrisoned. The
# `garrison` ai_strategy type, which parks divisions on states, is untouched as well.
