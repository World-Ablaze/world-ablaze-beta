# purpose: countries whose capital is not in Africa deprioritise the African areas toward net ~0
# range:   -90 per area (delta against the +100 baseline)
# policy:  Additive per area
# domain:  THEATRE
# note: relocated verbatim from legacy wa_default.txt DEFAULT_priority_non_africa (Phase 7c). The
#       GER/FRA exclusions predate the archetype rule and are kept as-is for behaviour parity: both
#       countries manage their own African priorities in their COUNTRY files.
