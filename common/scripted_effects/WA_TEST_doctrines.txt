#
#	Section B reads the nine tracks straight from the engine triggers (has_completed_track /
#	has_subdoctrine_in_track), never through the shipped effect or its observation trigger
#	(contract rule 4): `tier1-all-done` in the VERDICT line is re-derived here from the three
#	tier-1 readings, and `trigger-says-done` is the shipped WA_AI_DOCTRINES_has_completed_land_tier_1.
#	When the two disagree the fault is in one of them, not hidden between them.
#
#	READ THE VERDICT LINE AT THE BOTTOM. `tier1-all-done` and `trigger-says-done` must agree;
#	`gate-open` must read 1 exactly when the assist is meant to be pulsing.
############################################################################################################
