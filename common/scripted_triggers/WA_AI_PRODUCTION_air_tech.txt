# "should we act".
#
# Why these are WA_AI_ and not the plain WA_has_*_tech of WA_triggers.txt: a `has_worthwhile_*`
# trigger carries the AI's own judgement - the tree whose early models it refuses to spend a
# factory on. That judgement must never gate player-facing content, and the name is what stops a
# later author from wiring it there. The neutral "does this country hold the tech" lists that
# player content also reads (WA_has_fighter_tech, WA_has_heavy_fighter_tech,
# WA_has_tac_bomber_tech, read by HUN_scripted_triggers.txt) deliberately stay in WA_triggers.txt;
# the two wrappers at the bottom add the AI rung on top of them instead of copying the list.
